# Research context and references

Sources checked 23 October 2026. These references justify topic relevance and metric choices; they do not endorse this package.

## Current UK university research signals

1. **University of Edinburgh — Uncertainty-Aware Agentic AI.** The official research-theme page identifies uncertainty elicitation, message formats, propagation/aggregation and team-level calibration as research objectives. It says all international places for 2026 entry are filled; this package is not connected to that studentship.  
   https://eng.ed.ac.uk/spads-cdt/studying/degrees/postgraduate-research/phd/uncertainty-aware-agentic-ai

2. **University of Glasgow — agentic AI and software/data engineering partnership.** Published 30 March 2026; the university describes empirical evaluation of agentic AI effects on task quality and speed.  
   https://www.gla.ac.uk/news/headline_1256583_en.html

3. **University of Surrey — secure sandbox for AI agents.** Published 15 October 2026; the university reports a £3.2m collaboration on agent containment and permission controls. This does not mean this benchmark evaluates containment.  
   https://www.surrey.ac.uk/news/surrey-leads-ps32m-project-collaboration-sheffield-and-melbourne-develop-provably-secure-sandbox-ai

4. **University of Southampton — Responsibility gaps and AI safety in multiagent systems.** The official PhD-project page describes accountability under uncertainty and partial observability and lists a closing date of 30 November 2026. Check that page for current eligibility and funding details.  
   https://www.southampton.ac.uk/study/postgraduate-research/projects/responsibility-gaps-ai-safety-in-multiagent-systems

## Scope statement

The question is motivated by these published themes. This independently designed baseline is not a reproduction of a university project, and it does not claim that confidence-aware aggregation is novel in the broader literature.
