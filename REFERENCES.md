# References

Metric definitions used in this repository.

1. Brier, G. W. (1950). Verification of forecasts expressed in terms of probability. *Monthly Weather Review*, 78(1), 1-3. https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0493(1950)078%3C0001:VOFEIT%3E2.0.CO;2
2. Guo, C., Pleiss, G., Sun, Y. and Weinberger, K. Q. (2017). On calibration of modern neural networks. *Proceedings of the 34th International Conference on Machine Learning*, PMLR 70, 1321-1330. https://proceedings.mlr.press/v70/guo17a.html

The aggregation rules are standard baselines (majority vote, averaged confidence and summed log-odds under an independence assumption). This repository does not claim they are new.
